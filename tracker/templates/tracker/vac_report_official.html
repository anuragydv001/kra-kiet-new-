Introduction - KIET Deemed to be University is committed to providing students with opportunities for holistic development and enhancing their employability, professional competencies, interdisciplinary knowledge and lifelong learning skills.

In addition to the prescribed curriculum, the University offers Value Added Courses (VACs), Add-on Courses and Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) to provide students with exposure to emerging technologies, industry practices, professional skills, interdisciplinary learning and global learning resources.

• These courses are designed to complement the regular curriculum and contribute to the development of graduate attributes, employability skills and overall student development

Objectives of Value Added Courses - The major objectives of the Value Added Courses are:

  1. To supplement the knowledge acquired through the regular curriculum.
  2. To provide exposure to emerging technologies and industry practices.
  3. To develop employability and professional skills.
  4. To promote interdisciplinary and experiential learning.
